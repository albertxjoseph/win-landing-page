The idea

Everyone is connected.
Almost nobody is introduced.

Social apps optimise for reach. Professional apps optimise for résumés. Neither one gets you into a room with the person who could change your next twelve months. WINLIST is built for that introduction, and nothing else.

Matched by intent, not by algorithm

You see people who share your field and are within about 50 miles of you. Swipe right to connect, left to pass. No filters to game, no follower counts to chase, no feed deciding who deserves your attention.

Mentors you can actually book

Mentors and visionaries apply separately and carry a verified seal. Each one sets a flat session rate on their profile, and calls happen inside the app. You know the price before you ask, and they know you're serious.

Communities that leave the group chat

Join communities around what you're building. Members vote on a day, a time and a venue, and the meetup gets pinned. WINLIST curates events near you too. The point is to end up in the same room.

How it works

Four steps.
No cold outreach.

  1. 01

    Apply, don't sign up

    Everyone comes in through an application — as a Builder or as a Mentor. That's what keeps the room worth being in.

  2. 02

    Introduce yourself on video

    Your profile leads with a short video, not a headshot. People decide to connect with a person, not a job title.

  3. 03

    Connect, then talk

    Swipe through people in your field nearby. A mutual connect opens a real conversation — messages, calls, session bookings.

  4. 04

    Show up in person

    Communities and curated local events turn a connection into a coffee, a collaboration, or a hire.

Questions

Straight answers.

Is WINLIST free?

Joining, building a profile, connecting and messaging are free. A premium tier unlocks extras such as seeing who wants to connect with you before you swipe. Mentor sessions are paid separately, at the flat rate each mentor sets on their own profile.

Why is it invite-only?

Because the value of a network is the people in it. Every member — Builder or Mentor — comes through an application, so the person on the other side of a connect is someone who also chose to be here.

How is this different from LinkedIn?

LinkedIn is a résumé you broadcast at strangers. WINLIST is a room you get introduced into. No cold outreach, no connection-count race, and no feed rewarding whoever posts most — just people near you, in your field, who agreed to meet.

When does it launch?

The app is built and in testing now. We're opening one city at a time through 2026, starting where the interest list is densest — which is a real reason to add your city.

What happens to my information?

It's stored privately and used only to process your invite and tell you when your city opens. We don't sell it, share it, or add you to anything you didn't ask for. Reply to any message from us and we'll delete it.
